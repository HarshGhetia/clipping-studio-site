Clipping Studio

Clipping Studio runs on its owner's own computer. It finds the best moments in YouTube videos and live streams the owner is allowed to reuse, turns them into captioned Shorts and clips, and publishes them to the owner's own YouTube channels when the owner says so.

Who uses it

Only its owner. It isn't a public service: there are no sign-ups and no user accounts, and nothing runs on a server. On Google's sign-in screen it appears as “clipping-studio-yt”.

What it asks Google for, and why

  • Manage your YouTube videos (youtube.upload): to upload the clips the owner approves, and set their thumbnails.
  • See, edit and delete your YouTube content (youtube.force-ssl): to add a subtitle track, put a clip in a playlist the owner picked, post the owner's first comment under it, and read the view, like and comment counts of the videos it published.

It never deletes videos and never acts on a channel its owner didn't connect.
